About

Anna Semenoff

Anna was raised in Grand Forks, British Columbia and now works and lives in Kelowna, BC. Deeply influenced by the people around her, Anna's practice explores questions of the human condition. Working through abstract and figurative oil painting, she processes and reflects on personal narratives. As this inquiry continues, her work expands into larger-scale painting, where refined composition and physical movement become central to creating a distinct spatial presence around the work.

Available for commissions and studio visits by appointment.
